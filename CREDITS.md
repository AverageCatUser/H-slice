# Credits

## The engine

H-Slice — the engine (`index.html`), the mod builder (`mod-builder.html`) and
the documentation — is by **Elias / CyberMeow**, under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).

It's written from scratch. It reads Psych Engine's and the Funkin' Crew's chart
and spritesheet formats, but uses no code from either.

## The game

**Friday Night Funkin'** is by the **Funkin' Crew**:

- **ninjamuffin99** — programming
- **PhantomArcade** and **evilsk8r** — art and animation
- **Kawai Sprite** — music

Everything in `engineassets/weeks/`, and the arrows, icons, characters and menu
audio in `engineassets/`, are **their work, not mine**. They're included here so
the engine is playable out of the box, the same way other fan engines ship them.
They are **not** covered by this repository's MIT licence, and nothing here is
official or affiliated.

Play and support the original: <https://ninja-muffin24.itch.io/funkin>

If you're with the Funkin' Crew and want the assets taken down, open an issue or
get in touch and I'll remove them straight away — the engine runs fine without
them.

## Mods

Any song that isn't from the base game belongs to whoever charted, drew or
composed it. Mods made by other people aren't redistributed here — if you want
one, get it from its author.
